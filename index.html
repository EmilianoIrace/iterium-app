📋 Privacy Policy

Iterium does not collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All trip data is stored locally on your device.

  • Trip itineraries are saved only on your iPhone — never uploaded to our servers.
  • The app optionally connects to the Groq API using an API key you provide. This key is stored securely in the iOS Keychain and is never transmitted to third parties.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising SDKs are used.
  • No account or registration is required.
  • Map data is provided by Apple Maps (MapKit) under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Location photos are fetched from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons — no personal data is sent in these requests.
